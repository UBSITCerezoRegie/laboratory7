Welcome to the Pokémon Regions Application

Explore the Gym Leaders and favorite Pokémon from the Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn regions. You can also visit the Pokémart to purchase useful items and manage your shopping cart.

🏆 Kanto Region

Kanto is the first Pokémon region introduced in the series. It features iconic Gym Leaders such as Brock, Misty, Lt. Surge, and Giovanni. This application also showcases six favorite Pokémon from the Kanto region.

🥇 Johto Region

Johto was introduced in Pokémon Gold and Silver. Known for its rich history and traditions, it is home to Gym Leaders such as Falkner, Whitney, Morty, and Clair. Discover six favorite Pokémon from the Johto region.

🌊 Hoenn Region

Hoenn is famous for its tropical landscapes, vast oceans, and diverse environments. Meet Gym Leaders such as Roxanne, Brawly, Wattson, and Wallace while exploring six favorite Pokémon from the region.

🛒 Pokémart

Visit the Pokémart to purchase popular items such as Potions, Revives, Poké Balls, and more. Add items to your cart and view the total cost of your purchases.

Use the navigation bar above to browse each region and visit the Pokémart.
