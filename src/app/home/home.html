Welcome to the Gym Leaders Application

This application showcases Gym Leaders from two major Pokémon regions: Kanto and Johto.

Kanto Region

The Kanto region is the original Pokémon region introduced in the first generation games. It is known for its classic cities and the original 8 Gym Leaders that players must defeat to challenge the Pokémon League.

Johto Region

The Johto region was introduced in Pokémon Gold and Silver. It expands on Kanto’s world and features 8 new Gym Leaders with different types and battle styles.

Use the navigation bar above to explore each region’s Gym Leaders.
